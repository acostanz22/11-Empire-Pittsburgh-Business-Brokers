More Than 40 Years of Business Brokerage Experience

International Business Brokers has represented business buyers and sellers across America since 1981. We provide confidential, professional guidance to owners looking to value, market, and sell their businesses, as well as qualified buyers searching for the right acquisition opportunity.

Backed by decades of nationwide brokerage experience, our team helps clients navigate every stage of the transaction—from the initial consultation and valuation through negotiations, due diligence, and closing.

Pittsburgh Business Brokers serves owners and buyers throughout the greater Pittsburgh area, including Downtown, Oakland, the Strip District, Cranberry, South Hills, East End, and surrounding communities in Western Pennsylvania.
